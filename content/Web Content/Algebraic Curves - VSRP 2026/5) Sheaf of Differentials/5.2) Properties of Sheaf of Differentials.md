### Properties of Sheaf of Differentials

#### Properties of Sheaf of Differentials

The following are some general properties of the sheaf of differentials.

> [!bluebox] **Proposition 5.2.1**: (Hartshorne II 8.10)
> Let $f: X\to Y$ be a morphism of schemes, let $g: Y' \to Y$ be another morphism and let $f': X'= X\times_YY' \to Y'$ be obtained by base extension. Then $\Omega_{X'/Y'} \simeq g'^*(\Omega_{X/Y})$ where $g': X'\to X$ is the first projection.

> [!bluebox] **Proposition 5.2.2**: (Hartshorne II 8.11)
> Let $f: X\to Y$ and $g: Y\to Z$ be morphisms of schemes, Then there is an exact sequence of sheaves on $X$,
>
> $$
> f^*\Omega_{Y/Z} \to \Omega_{X/Z}\to \Omega_{X/Y}\to 0
> $$

^6dc98b

> [!bluebox] **Proposition 5.2.3**: (Hartshorne II 8.12)
> Let $f: X\to Y$ be a morphism of schemes and let $Z$ be a closed subscheme of $X$, with ideal sheaf $\SI$. Then there is an exact sequence of sheaves on $Z$,
>
> $$
> \SI/\SI^2\to \Omega_{X/Y}\otimes \SO_Z \to \Omega_{Z/Y}
> $$

#### Sheaf of Differentials for varieties

> [!orangebox]+ **Theorem 5.2.4**: (Hartshorne II 8.7)
> Let $A$ be a local ring that contains a field $k$ isomorphic to its residue field $B/\mathfrak m$. Then the map $\delta: \mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2 \to \Omega_{A/k}\otimes_B k$ obtained from the second exact sequence, is an isomorphism.

^4d1983

Note the map $\delta$ sends $\bar a$ to $da\otimes 1$

> [!orangebox]+ **Theorem 5.2.5**: (Hartshorne II 8.8)
> Let $B$ be a local ring containing a field $k$ isomorphic to its residue field. Assume furthermore that $k$ is perfect, and that $B$ is a localization of a finitely generated $k$-algebra. Then $\Omega_{B/k}$ is a free module of rank equal to $\Dim B$ if and only if $B$ is a regular local ring.

In the proof of this theorem we see that a basis for $\Omega_{B/k}\otimes_Bk$ lifts to a basis of $\Omega_{B/k}$. Using this, we get the following.

> [!greenbox]+ **Corollary 5.2.6**:
> Let $X$ be a curve,  i.e. a one dimensional and $x\in X$ be smooth closed point. Then $\OXx$ is a DVR and let $t$ be a local parameter at $x$, i.e. a generator for the maximal ideal of $\OXx$. Then $(\Omega_{X/k})_x = \Omega_{\OXx/k}$ is a free $\OXx$-module of rank one with $dt$ as a basis.

> [!proof]- _Proof_:
> From the remark above, a generator for $\Omega_{\OXx/k}\otimes_{\OXx}k$ would lift to a basis of $\Omega_{\OXx/k}$.
> From [[#^4d1983|Theorem]] 5.2.4, we may use any non-zero element of $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$ as a generator above. And since $\OXx$ is a DVR, $t\in \mathfrak m - \mathfrak m^2$. Thus $0 \neq \bar t \mapsto dt\otimes 1$ which lifts to $dt$. Thus $dt$ generates $\Omega_{\OXx/k}$

> [!orangebox]+ **Theorem 5.2.7**: (Hartshorne II 8.15)
> Let $X$ be an irreducible separated scheme of finite type over an algebraically closed $k$. Then $\Omega_{X/k}$ is locally free of rank $n = \Dim X$ if and only if $X$ is a non-singular variety over $k$.

> [!greenbox]+ **Corollary 5.2.8**:
> If $X$ is non-singular curve, then $\Omega_{X/k}$ is a locally free sheaf of rank $1$, thus it is a line bundle.

^b82e06

> [!proof] _Proof_:
> Direct consequence of [[#^aa2836|Theorem]] 5.2.7.
